About

I am an artificial intelligence and machine learning engineer who builds systems that people actually get to use, not only notebooks that stop at a demo. Research and featured projects

The two pieces of work I would point a recruiter to first, one on healthcare, one on language.

Non invasive haemoglobin level detection

Personal research project, 2024

A comparison of five machine learning models, Linear Regression, SVM, MLP, DNN and CNN, for predicting blood haemoglobin level from four wavelength PPG signals rather than a blood draw. I built the full pipeline myself, from raw signal feature extraction through ReliefF feature selection to Particle Swarm Optimisation for hyperparameter tuning, and found that a well tuned SVM matched or beat far more expensive deep learning models on this small, noisy, real world medical dataset.
